I am a Master's student studying Artificial Intelligence. I enjoy thinking about and building AI systems that help people. Right now, I'm particularly interested in building and evaluating AI systems that enhance people's decision making and efficiency while combating their long-term side effects, such as skill and dignity degradation and review fatigue.

**What I'm working on**
- **Nephrology diagnostic support** ([Ananas research group](https://www.anamarasovic.com/mentoring/)): I benchmark and interpret LLMs for tools that support nephrologists in diagnosis.
- **Co-founder, [Scattershot AI](https://scattershot-ai.github.io/webpage/)**: I'm co-founding a startup building a multi-agent system that augments the penetration testing workflow. I design the multi-agent-human interactions for this safety-critical domain and build the agents themselves.

**Previously**
- **Smart rehabilitation** ([PeDEL lab](https://pedel.cs.utah.edu/)): I worked with healthcare providers and patients in a smart rehabilitation hospital to develop design considerations for better supporting people's needs with technology.
