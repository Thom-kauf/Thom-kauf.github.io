I am a Master's student studying Artificial Intelligence. I enjoy thinking about and building AI systems that help people. Right now, I'm particularly interested in building and evaluating AI systems that enhance people's decision making and efficiency while combating their long-term side effects, such as review fatigue and the erosion of skill and dignity.

I'm graduating in December 2026 and am looking for research and industry roles where I can keep building and studying AI systems that make people more capable.

**What I'm working on**
- **Nephrology diagnostic support** ([Ananas research group](https://www.anamarasovic.com/mentoring/)): I'm benchmarking and interpreting LLMs for tools that support nephrologists in diagnosis.
- **Co-founder, [Scattershot AI](https://scattershot-ai.github.io/webpage/)**: I'm co-founding a startup building a multi-agent system that augments the penetration testing workflow. I'm designing the multi-agent-human interactions for this safety-critical domain and building the agents themselves.

**Previously**
- **Smart rehabilitation** ([PeDEL lab](https://pedel.cs.utah.edu/)): I worked with healthcare providers and patients in a smart rehabilitation hospital to develop design considerations for better supporting their needs with technology.
